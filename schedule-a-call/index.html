02 · Who this is for

What I help people with

Panic attacks DP/DR & feeling unreal Health anxiety & checking Avoidance & agoraphobia Overstimulation & stuck emotions

Most of the people I work with look fine from the outside. Inside, they are managing a body that keeps sounding the alarm. They check their pulse and their breathing. They avoid driving, stores, or being far from home. They feel unreal, or overstimulated, or cut off from the person they used to be.

Many have already been to doctors and heard that everything looks normal. Some have done therapy. The tests rule out danger, and the fear stays.

That gap is where my work lives.

We look at what is actually driving the fear, and we work with it directly.

The goal is for you to feel like yourself again.

03 · About Enis

I went through this myself

My name is Enis Cirak. I had panic attacks, depersonalization, and bad anxiety for about four or five years. I planned my days around whether my body would cooperate. I sat in rooms full of people feeling like the only one who could not just be there.

Turning toward the feelings is what finally worked. Getting out took years of study and practice, and then it became my work. I now coach people through panic, DP/DR, avoidance, and the emotions underneath them, one-on-one.

Doctors were important. They ruled out the dangerous explanations. But ruling out danger did not teach me how to get my life back. That second part is what I help people with, and it is what the free call is about.

We go at your pace. No rushing and no pushing.

06 · Questions

Before you book

Is this a sales call?

It is a real conversation about what is happening to you. At the end, if it seems like a fit, I will explain how working together goes. If it does not, I will tell you that too. Either way, you leave with a clearer picture of your pattern.

What if I get anxious before or during the call?

That is normal, and you would not be the first. It is a phone call, so there is no camera, and you can say out loud that you are anxious the moment it happens. That is allowed here.

Is this therapy?

No. This is coaching. It is not therapy, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency care. Many of my clients work with me alongside therapy or medication.

What does coaching cost after the free call?

Sessions are $125 for 60 minutes, booked one at a time. No packages and no contracts. The free call carries no obligation either way.

What should I prepare?

Nothing. Come as you are, even if you do not have the words for what is happening yet. Finding the words together is part of the call.
