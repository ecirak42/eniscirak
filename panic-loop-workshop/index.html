The pattern

The symptom is scary. The fear of the symptom is what keeps the loop alive.

A wave of adrenaline hits.

Your heart races. Your chest tightens. You feel dizzy, hot, shaky, unreal, disconnected, or flooded with fear.

Then the mind tries to protect you: “What if something is wrong?” “What if I lose control?” “What if this feeling never goes away?”

That fear sends another danger signal through the body. More adrenaline comes. The sensations get louder. Then you start checking, resisting, avoiding, scanning, and bracing.

Before long, you are not only afraid of panic. You are afraid of your own inner experience. That is the loop this workshop is designed to help you understand.

Why I teach this

I’m not teaching this from theory alone.

My name is Enis Cirak. I lived inside this loop for years.

Panic attacks. Depersonalization. Intense anxiety. The quiet habit of scanning my body, planning around sensations, and trying to make sure I would not get “triggered” at the wrong time.

That experience changed how I talk about anxiety.

I do not see panic as weakness. I do not see it as a character flaw. And I do not think the answer is to force yourself harder, shame yourself into exposure, or try to positive-think your way out of something your body is deeply reacting to.

I see panic as a pattern your body can learn. It involves adrenaline, thoughts, sensations, emotional pressure, avoidance, and the way you respond to what is happening inside you.

This workshop is here to help you understand the pattern and respond with a little more steadiness.

FAQ

Questions people often ask

Is this therapy?

No. This is a coaching workshop for education and practice. It can complement therapy or personal growth work, but it does not replace clinical care if that is what you need.

Will this stop my panic attacks?

No one can promise a specific outcome. The goal is to help you understand the panic loop, reduce fear around symptoms, and begin practicing responses that may help the pattern soften over time.

Is this good for depersonalization/derealization?

DP/DR will be discussed as part of the panic/anxiety loop. The focus is on understanding why unreality feelings can show up when the nervous system feels overloaded, and how to relate to those sensations with less fear and resistance.

What if I can’t attend live?

A replay is included, so you can watch afterward if you cannot attend live.

What is included with the VIP ticket?

The VIP ticket includes the live workshop, worksheet, replay, guided practice, and one 60-minute private Anxiety Pattern Session.

Is this for agoraphobia?

The workshop touches on avoidance and agoraphobia patterns, especially how avoidance can grow and how exposure can be approached more gently. For severe or disabling agoraphobia, it can be wise to work with licensed support too.

Do I need meditation experience?

No. The practices will be simple, beginner-friendly, and grounded.

What happens after the workshop?

After the workshop, you can continue with a private session, ongoing coaching, or community practice if you want more support. There is no pressure to choose anything beyond the workshop.
