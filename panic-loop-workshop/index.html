The panic loop

The symptom is scary. The fear of the symptom keeps the loop alive.

A wave of adrenaline hits. Your heart races, your chest tightens, you feel dizzy, shaky, unreal, or flooded with fear.

Then the mind tries to protect you: “What if something is wrong?” That fear sends another danger signal through the body. More adrenaline comes. The sensations get louder.

FAQ

Questions people often ask

Is this therapy?

No. This is a coaching workshop for education and practice. It can complement therapy or personal growth work, but it does not replace clinical care if that is what you need.

Will this stop my panic attacks?

No one can promise a specific outcome. The goal is to help you understand the panic loop, reduce fear around symptoms, and begin practicing responses that may help the pattern soften over time.

Is this good for depersonalization/derealization?

Yes, DP/DR is part of the workshop. We’ll talk about why unreality feelings can show up when the nervous system feels overloaded, and how to meet them with less fear.
