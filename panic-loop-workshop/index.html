The pattern

If panic feels random, your nervous system may be caught in a loop.

Panic can feel like it comes out of nowhere. A strange sensation appears. Your mind tries to solve it quickly. The worry itself can bring more adrenaline, and adrenaline can create more sensations.

Over time, many people start monitoring, resisting, checking, avoiding, and bracing. That understandable protection strategy can make the whole system more sensitive, sometimes showing up as more anxiety, depersonalization/derealization, and avoidance.

Why I teach this

I know how convincing panic can feel from the inside.

My name is Enis Cirak. I lived with panic attacks, depersonalization, and intense anxiety for years. It changed the way I moved through ordinary life: social situations, meetings, trying new foods, and even being in my own body could start to feel loaded.

I remember what it is like to look fine on the outside while quietly scanning your sensations, planning around fear, and wondering why your nervous system will not just settle down.

This workshop comes from that lived experience, along with years of studying meditation, somatic awareness, cognitive tools, nervous-system education, and compassionate inner work. It is not about pushing yourself harder. It is about understanding the loop clearly enough that you can begin responding to it in a different way.

The tone of this workshop is practical and human: clear education, simple practices, and room for the part of you that is tired of fighting your own body.

FAQ

Questions people often ask

Is this therapy?

No. This is an educational coaching workshop. It is not therapy, diagnosis, treatment, crisis support, or medical advice.

Will this stop my panic attacks?

No one can ethically guarantee that. The goal is to help you understand the loop, reduce fear around symptoms, and build practices that may help you respond differently over time.

Is this good for depersonalization/derealization?

The workshop includes discussion of DP/DR as a common experience when the nervous system feels highly activated or overwhelmed. The focus is on understanding it, reducing fear around it, and building grounding/regulation practices.

No. The practices will be simple and beginner-friendly.
