## Email 2 - Day Before The Workshop

Subject: Tomorrow: what actually keeps panic going

Preview text: A simple thing to notice before we meet.

Body:

Hi {$name},

The Panic Loop Workshop is tomorrow at 11 AM Central / 12 PM Eastern.

Here is the main idea we will build from:

Panic usually does not keep going because the first sensation is dangerous. It keeps going because the body has learned to treat the sensation like a warning sign.

So the moment something shows up - a racing heart, dizziness, unreality, heat, chest tightness, shaky legs - the mind starts checking:

What is this?

Is something wrong?

What if this gets worse?

What if I panic here?

That checking makes sense. But it often tells the nervous system, "This matters. Keep watching."

Then adrenaline rises, the symptoms get louder, and the whole thing feels more convincing.

Tomorrow we are going to look at this in a way that is practical and very human. Not just "change your thoughts." Not "calm down." Not forcing yourself through terror.

We are going to look at what you do after the first sensation appears, because that is where the loop can start to change.

## Email 6 - Next Day

Subject: The old way and the new way

Preview text: This is the part people usually miss.

Body:

Hi {$name},

One thing I see a lot:

People think they have not made progress because they have not found the perfect technique yet.

But very often, the deeper issue is that the whole relationship to anxiety is still based on resistance.

The old way is:

Make the sensation stop so I can be okay.

The new way is:

Let me learn how to be with this sensation without turning it into an emergency.

That does not mean you like it. It does not mean you force yourself. It does not mean you pretend it is easy.

It means you start relating to the experience differently.

When you stop fighting every wave, the body has a chance to process what it has been holding. The stuck fear, pressure, and emotional energy can start to move instead of staying locked in place.

Preview text: You do not need more random tips. You need to see your pattern clearly.

Body:

Hi {$name},

If the panic loop is still running, it does not mean you missed something obvious.

Most people do not stay stuck because they are weak.

They stay stuck because the nervous system has learned a pattern:

sensation -> fear -> more adrenaline -> checking or escape -> short relief -> more fear next time.

And once that pattern is trained, it can start showing up everywhere:

driving, stores, meetings, restaurants, being alone, being far from home, eating new foods, sitting still, trying to sleep.

The way out is usually not one giant breakthrough.

It is learning, again and again, how to respond differently when the alarm turns on.

## Email 8 - One Week Later

Subject: A question that changes the work

Preview text: This is a useful place to look.

Body:

Hi {$name},

Here is a question I like:

What do I do when I start to feel something I do not want to feel?

That question matters because panic and anxiety are not only about symptoms. They are also about the way the system reacts to symptoms.

Do you scan?

Do you tense?

Do you try to solve it immediately?

Do you look for reassurance?

Do you leave?

Do you get angry at yourself for feeling it again?

None of that means you did anything wrong. It means the body has been trying to protect you in the only way it knows.

But protection can become a cage.

The work is learning how to open that cage gently, without forcing yourself and without obeying every alarm.
