02 · The work

What sessions are like

Sessions are calm and personal. We talk directly. There is no need to perform or to say the right thing.

We work with whatever is going on for you right now. Some sessions are about understanding exactly how your panic works. Others are about sitting with the fear in your body instead of fighting it, or finding the deeper emotion underneath it.

The goal is for panic to stop running your life.

04 · About Enis

I went through this myself

My name is Enis Cirak. I had panic attacks, depersonalization, and bad anxiety for about four or five years.

It changed how I lived. I avoided social situations and meetings. I got paranoid about trying new foods. I was overstimulated most of the time and did not feel like myself.

I know what it is like to plan a day around whether your body will cooperate. I know what it is like to sit in a room full of people and feel like the only one who cannot just be there.

Turning toward the feelings is what finally worked. The sensations still came at first, but I stopped treating every sensation like a problem I had to solve immediately.

Getting out took years. I studied everything I could find on panic and the nervous system, and I tried it all on myself first. Now I coach people through panic, DP/DR, and avoidance one-on-one.

We go at your pace. No rushing and no pushing.

Influences: Internal Family Systems, Michael Singer, Eckhart Tolle, Adyashanti, David Burns.

07 · FAQ

Common questions

Is this therapy?

No. This is coaching. It is not therapy, and it is not a medical service.

Do I need to have agoraphobia for this to help?

No. This work can also help if you have panic attacks, fear of fear, ongoing anxiety, or avoidance that is starting to shape your life.

Do you use exposure?

Not in a pushy or rigid way. Exposure helps a lot of people. If it is part of what you are doing, this work can go alongside it. What I focus on is understanding the pattern, and changing how you relate to what is happening underneath.

What happens in a session?

We talk about what is happening, but we do not just stop at talking. We slow down to notice what is going on in your body, your mind, and your emotions, as it happens. Then we work with what is actually driving the fear.

Can I still work with you if I am in therapy or on medication?

Yes. Many people are. This work can sit alongside therapy or medication. Medication decisions stay between you and your doctor.

How long does it take?

It depends on the person and the pattern. Some people feel clearer quickly. For others it takes longer. The point is to do real work that actually helps, not to force a timeline.
